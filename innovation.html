Our Journey

Three Decades of Innovation

1994

Founded in Precision District, TX

Established with a vision to bring precision engineering to the power transformer industry.

2002

First Export — Middle East Grid Project

Delivered 50 MVA transformers for a major substation expansion in the UAE, marking international expansion.

2010

ISO 9001 & 14001 Dual Certification

Achieved simultaneous quality and environmental management certification, underscoring our commitment to excellence.

2017

Innovation Lab Inaugurated

Dedicated R&D facility established with 40+ engineers focused on next-generation power solutions.
